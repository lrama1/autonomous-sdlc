Trailmates · SDLC platform example

The main example. A founder and two engineers build a hiking-trip planning app with agents. Three checkpoints by default, people wearing several hats, routine changes on autopilot, and a queue that brings people in only when their judgment matters. Everything is sample data. Other examples: Kestrel Market (an online store). Parked: compliance pack.
