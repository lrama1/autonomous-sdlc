Kestrel Market · SDLC platform example (online store)

Core example. An online store with customer journeys and internal employee tools, built with Balanced autonomy. The money path gets a closer look: payment-scope triggers always bring in the Architect and Security, a QA verification checkpoint is switched on, a release calendar holds normal releases during the change freeze, and a hotfix path covers peak-season emergencies.
