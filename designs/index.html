SDLC Platform · Screen designs

A semi-autonomous SDLC where AI agents and people build software together, and people are brought in at the moments their judgment matters. These are clickable mockups with sample data. Use the persona switcher in the top bar, and click through.

Two core examples: Trailmates (a small team's hiking app: the full loop, plus the human-injection screens) · Kestrel Market (an online store with customer and employee work, a QA checkpoint, and a release calendar).

Prefer to watch? Trailmates end-to-end · Kestrel end-to-end · all videos

The heart of the product: a ranked attention queue where every ask carries its context, options and a recommendation, answers from Slack, assumptions and standing decisions, and the numbers for tuning how often agents interrupt.
